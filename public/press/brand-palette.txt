Margot — brand palette

Background
  Cream            #ECEAE5    primary canvas
  Surface          #F6F4EF    cards, secondary surfaces
  Warm divider     #CECABF    hairlines, borders

Type
  Ink              #2D3A33    primary text, the dark green-charcoal
  Ink subtle       #4D5852    secondary body
  Ink muted        #5C6660    captions, eyebrows

Accent
  Peach            #A84A2D    the period in "Margot.", small-caps eyebrows
  Sage             #5F7560    occasional warm accent
  Rust             #A0421F    deeper alternative to peach

Typefaces
  Display          Fraunces (variable, opsz axis; italic carries emphasis)
  Body             Inter Tight (400–700)

Notes
  The peach period after "Margot" is non-negotiable brand detail.
  Italic Fraunces is for warmth and emphasis; regular Fraunces is for body of headlines.
  Never use Fraunces upright for small caps (use Inter Tight 600 instead).
